# 🎛️ SPN-045 Restore the rail's theme and profile buttons from the mockup

## Story

As the [Owner](../product/personas.md#owner), I want the theme switch and my avatar at the bottom of the rail, as in the gem-rail mockup, so that I can change themes and reach my profile without hunting through Settings. [Collaborators](../product/personas.md#collaborator) get the same.

## Scenarios

### Choosing the theme

Given any signed-in page following the system theme,
When I use the rail's theme switch,
Then I choose System, Light or Dark in a three-segment control with a visible selected state,
And the choice persists across reloads, the same as the picker on Your profile.

### Opening my profile

Given I'm signed in,
When I select my avatar at the bottom of the rail,
Then **Your profile** opens with my passkeys and the theme picker that Settings has today.
