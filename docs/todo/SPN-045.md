# 🎛️ SPN-045 Restore the rail's theme and profile buttons from the mockup

## Story

As the [Owner](../product/personas.md#owner), I want the theme switch and my avatar at the bottom of the rail, as in the gem-rail mockup, so that I can change themes and reach my profile without hunting through Settings. [Collaborators](../product/personas.md#collaborator) get the same.

## Scenarios

### Cycling the theme

Given any signed-in page following the system theme,
When I use the rail's theme switch,
Then it moves system → light → dark → system, shows the current state's icon (monitor, sun, moon) and names it in its hover/focus label,
And the choice persists across reloads, the same as the picker on Your profile.

### Opening my profile

Given I'm signed in,
When I select my avatar at the bottom of the rail,
Then **Your profile** opens with my passkeys and the theme picker that Settings has today.

## Rules

- Restores what SPN-027 dropped from the reference: in `30a4a61^:docs/design/gem-rail-mockup.tsx`, the rail ends with a theme button and an avatar button that opens Your profile. Owner, chat 2026-10-19.
- Three states, cycled in the style of tailwindcss.com's theme switch. Confirm the form with the owner when starting. Owner, chat 2026-10-19.
- The avatar replaces the Settings rail link, and the `/settings` page becomes Your profile. Keep `/settings` working as a redirect. Grooming, 2026-10-19.
- One theme state: reuse `src/ui/ThemeSettings.tsx`'s storage and apply logic. The admin gets both buttons. Both are icon-only with accessible names.
- The avatar uses initials or `user-round` until SPN-036 adds the saved name, colour and icon to Your profile. This isn't a dependency.

- Preserve Connected assistants, named connections with last-used dates, Disconnect, and advanced personal access tokens when Settings moves to Your profile; retain pending OAuth sign-in/consent navigation (SPN-034; grooming, 2026-10-20).

## Out of scope

- The profile's name, colour and icon fields (SPN-036). Other rail changes ([design playbook](../design/README.md#shell-layout)).

## Verify

- Playwright: cycle through all three states and reload after each; open Your profile from the avatar; check `/settings` redirects. Repeat as the admin and at phone width.
