# 🔁 SPN-156 Sync saved edits while the file list is open

## Story

As an [Owner](../product/personas.md#owner), I want my saved edits to reach collaborators when my phone reconnects while I browse my files, so that I do not need to reopen every edited document to finish syncing.

## Scenarios

### Reconnect without an editor

Given a downloaded Doc or Sheet has saved offline edits and the file list is open,
When the running app reconnects or returns to the foreground online,
Then those edits reach the server once and their pending state clears after acknowledgement,
And another signed-in device sees them without reopening the editor on the first device.

### Authority, contention and interrupted replay remain safe

Given the account or access changes, another tab owns the item writer, or upload is interrupted,
When background replay is considered,
Then it respects the account and item authority and exclusive writer,
And denied or incompatible work remains recoverable without sending it as another account,
And saved outbox/snapshot state survives interruption, retry and conflict without duplicate edits.

## Out of scope

- OS background execution after the app is closed; periodic server polling; changing offline image or creation rules; iPhone acceptance (SPN-152).
