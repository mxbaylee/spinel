# 🌐 SPN-038 Access Spinel over public HTTPS without Tailscale

## Story

As the [Owner](../product/personas.md#owner), I want to open Spinel with HTTPS and my passkey without connecting a VPN, so that access works on my everyday devices.

## Scenarios

### Opening Spinel without Tailscale

Given a device outside the tailnet,
When I open `https://spinel.baylee.dev`,
Then HTTPS presents the passkey sign-in page,
And an authenticated everyday account can use its permitted documents,
And visitors without an account or valid guest link cannot discover or read workspace data,
And valid guest links retain access to only their shared document.

### Changing hostname

Given passkeys registered for an earlier hostname, such as localhost,
When the operator follows the hostname-change guide, recovering the admin from the console (SPN-061) and sending recovery invites,
Then everyone re-enrolls without being locked out,
And documents and account identities remain intact.

## Rules

- Last in the backlog: impact S, effort L, because it needs the owner working alongside an agent and can never run autonomously, and most features matter more while Spinel is hard to use. Owner, chat 2026-10-19.
- Public access is separate work from SPN-029; retain its encrypted storage and private database/management access. Owner, chat 2026-10-17.
- The owner chose `spinel.baylee.dev` and will configure DNS with guided steps. The private test host was retired; allocate new resources and recheck A/AAAA records rather than reusing old IPs. Owner, chat 2026-10-17.
- Include A/AAAA records, domain ownership, certificate issuance/renewal, HTTPS/WSS proxy configuration, IPv4/IPv6 firewall rules, exact APP_ORIGIN/WebAuthn RP settings and rollback.
- Review internet-facing authentication and enrollment: rate limits, challenge/session abuse, origin checks, cookie settings and credential recovery. Enabling HTTPS does not grant additional account or document access. Follow SPN-062's patch routine before exposure.
- Weigh the Operator's manageable attack surface, Owner's VPN-free access, Collaborator's credential continuity and Link guest's isolation. Preserve the landed guest-link isolation in docs/sharing.md (SPN-016).

## Out of scope

- Public signup, password login, changing guest-sharing semantics, and replacing the VPN used for server management.

## Verify

- From outside Tailscale: sign in and edit over HTTPS; verify signed-out, cross-account and admin/document boundaries, including hostile Origin and excessive authentication attempts; verify existing guest HTTPS/WSS, images and link rotation/revocation through the proxy.
- Verify TLS and intended public ports on IPv4/IPv6; demonstrate guided DNS/certificate setup, credential cutover, data preservation and rollback. Originating work: SPN-029 / PR #18 and owner chat.
