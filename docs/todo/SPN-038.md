# 🌐 SPN-038 Access Spinel over public HTTPS without Tailscale

## Story

As the [Owner](../product/personas.md#owner), I want to open Spinel with HTTPS and my passkey without connecting a VPN, so that access works on my everyday devices.

## Scenarios

### Opening Spinel without Tailscale

Given a device outside the tailnet,
When I open `https://spinel.baylee.dev`,
Then HTTPS presents the passkey sign-in page,
And an authenticated everyday account can use its permitted documents,
And signed-out visitors cannot discover or read workspace data.

### Moving from the private hostname

Given passkeys registered for localhost or the old tailnet hostname,
When the operator follows the cutover guide,
Then new-domain enrollment or recovery is completed without locking anyone out,
And documents and account identities remain intact.

## Rules

- Public access is separate work from SPN-029; retain its encrypted storage and private database/management access. Owner, chat 2026-10-17.
- The owner chose `spinel.baylee.dev` (correcting `spinel.mxbaylee.dev`) and will configure DNS with guided steps. Owner, chat 2026-10-17.
- The owner retired the disposable private test host and volume after acceptance; redeploy later with this public HTTPS work. Allocate new resources and recheck/update A/AAAA records rather than reusing old IPs. Public certificate, enrollment and cutover remain pending. Owner, chat 2026-10-17.
- Include A/AAAA records, domain ownership, certificate issuance/renewal, HTTPS/WSS proxy configuration, IPv4/IPv6 firewall rules, exact APP_ORIGIN/WebAuthn RP settings and rollback.
- Review internet-facing authentication and enrollment: rate limits, challenge/session abuse, origin checks, cookie settings and credential recovery. No signup or document access becomes public by enabling HTTPS.
- Weigh the Operator's manageable attack surface, Owner's VPN-free access, Collaborator's credential continuity and Link guest's isolation. Guest sharing remains its own ticket.
- Passkeys are bound to the relying-party domain; existing localhost/tailnet credentials are not assumed portable. Explain when to enroll replacements.

## Out of scope

- Public signup, password login, guest sharing, and replacing the VPN used for server management.

## Verify

- From outside Tailscale: sign in and edit over HTTPS; verify signed-out, cross-account and admin/document boundaries, including hostile Origin and excessive authentication attempts.
- Verify TLS and intended public ports on IPv4/IPv6; demonstrate guided DNS/certificate setup, credential cutover, data preservation and rollback. Originating work: SPN-029 / PR #18 and owner chat.
