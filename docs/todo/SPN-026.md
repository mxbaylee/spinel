# 🎚️ SPN-026 Browse today's history on a timeline and older days on a calendar

## Story

As the [Owner](../product/personas.md#owner), I want to scrub through today's changes on a slider and pick older days from a calendar, so that I find the version I want quickly.

## Scenarios

### Today

Given snapshots from today,
When I open history,
Then a timeline shows each snapshot, and dragging previews each one.

### Older days

Given daily snapshots from earlier,
When I switch to the calendar,
Then days with a snapshot are highlighted, and named versions are marked,
And choosing a day previews it.

## Rules

- Two views, as in PRD §5. Previews use SPN-025's preview.
- Works with touch on a phone (SPN-028).

## Out of scope

- Restoring (SPN-025).

## Verify

- Playwright: scrub today's timeline, pick a past day.
