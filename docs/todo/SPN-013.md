# 🚦 SPN-013 Queue editors when collaboration is full

## Story

As a [Collaborator](../product/personas.md#collaborator), I want to be told plainly when I can't edit yet because the collaboration limit is reached, and to get in when a place frees up, so that I wait instead of thinking Spinel is broken.

## Scenarios

### Too many editors

Given a document already has the most editors Univer allows,
When I open it,
Then I see it read-only on a queue page or banner that says who is editing and where I am in line,
And I can edit as soon as a place frees up, without reloading.

### Too many live documents

Given the instance is at its limit of documents open for co-editing,
When I open another,
Then I'm told whether I can edit alone or must wait, matching the limit SPN-010 measured.

## Rules

- Keep this optional, low impact and high effort while capacity evidence is inconclusive. The short SPN-010 matrix found no rejection at its tested bounds; that does not prove no limit exists. Owner, chat 2026-10-17; #27.
- Build only after a reproducible limit Spinel can reach is recorded in the collaboration findings. Retain this ticket if bounded tests find no limit; this supersedes automatic retirement in #23. Owner, chat 2026-10-17; #27.
- Show the limits in the product instead of hiding them. Owner, chat 2026-10-17.
- The numbers come from `docs/architecture/collaboration.md`, with one config value for each.
- Weighs the Collaborator and the Owner equally: whoever opened the document first keeps editing.

## Out of scope

- Removing the limits (SPN-014).

## Verify

- Unit tests for the queue: joining, leaving, promotion order.
- Playwright: open with one more editor than the limit and watch the queue promote.
