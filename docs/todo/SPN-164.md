# ⏱️ SPN-164 Shorten the sign-in check wait after reconnecting

## Story

As the [Owner](../product/personas.md#owner), I want the app to finish its sign-in check promptly after my phone reconnects, so that I am not left waiting on a status line.

## Scenarios

### Reconnecting does not wait out the full timeout

Given the app is open and the phone was offline,
When connectivity returns,
Then the sign-in check completes without waiting the full 15 seconds for a request that cannot answer.

## Rules

- Investigation and persona decisions: [SPN-164 record](../implementation/SPN-164.md). Owner explicitly accepts local reproduction in place of physical-phone acceptance for this ticket, chat 2026-11-22; [waiver and limits](../implementation/SPN-164.md#-physical-phone-waiver-and-local-acceptance). No other gate is waived.
- Follow [sustainable testing](../testing.md); keep the Verify plan and progress evidence current. [Policy](../implementation/SPN-115.md#guidance-for-every-open-ticket-and-progress-checkpoint), owner 2026-11-01.

- Observed in the SPN-156 own-phone run, 2026-11-16 (owner): Pixel 10, Chrome 157.0.8103.78, installed app, build `ccc201ef` of [PR 289](https://github.com/mxbaylee/spinel/pull/289). After turning airplane mode off, the owner saw about 15 s on the **sign-in check** startup stage and expected it reduced. Not confirmed that this is the path hit, and not reproduced.
- Source of the 15 s: `web/api.ts` gives the `session` request `AbortSignal.timeout(15_000)`; the stage is `startupStage('sign-in check')` in `web/App.tsx`.
- What earlier work reduced: only `navigator.onLine === false`, which skips the wait (`web/App.tsx`). With airplane mode just off, `onLine` is true but the network may not be ready, so the request can still wait out the timeout. See the third cold-launch run in [SPN-090 own-phone findings](../implementation/SPN-090-own-phone-findings.md).
- First observe: log timestamps for the stage and the request on a phone that has just reconnected. Then decide between a shorter session timeout while recovering from offline and another readiness signal, recording the tradeoff for all four personas. A slow server must not be mistaken for an outage that signs people out.

## Out of scope

- Changing offline-start behavior when the browser reports offline.

## Verify

- Contract/negative case: after a reconnect the check completes within the chosen bound, and a genuinely slow server is not treated as signed-out; suite/layer/fixture: unit test of the session request and outage classification with a controllable clock.
- Fixture readiness/cleanup: fake timers and a stubbed request; no real network.
- Environment and source/shared dependencies: `web/api.ts`, `web/App.tsx`, `web/startupStage.ts`.
- Evidence: recorded local reconnect stage/request timings before and after; physical phone not run under the ticket-specific owner waiver above. Preserve original failures and the historical cause uncertainty.
