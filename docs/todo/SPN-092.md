# 🪢 SPN-092 Share Spaces with guests and verify integrations

## Story

As the [Owner](../product/personas.md#owner), I want to share a whole Space without leaking unrelated information, so that [Link guests](../product/personas.md#link-guest) can use its contents while formulas, recovery and existing integrations keep their access guarantees.

## Scenarios

### One guest link covers one Space

Given a View or Edit guest link to a Space,
When the guest opens any current or newly added page, Sheet, image or same-Space formula source,
Then every child follows that Space role, Edit can manage its current content/tree, and no child grant/link, outer folder, account identity or account-only recovery/history control is exposed.

### Formulas keep source permissions

Given a Doc inside a Space uses scalar, range or multi-source formulas,
When it renders or its permissions change,
Then SPN-033 syntax, stable bindings, formatting and unavailable states remain intact, and every external source still requires independent access; the Space link grants none outside its boundary.

## Rules

- Complete guest capability, collaboration, image, Formula, MCP and existing export integration using current services; recheck Space scope at every HTTP/socket/MCP/export boundary, including after async work. Revocation, rotation, downgrade, trash and disabled owners affect open editors and subsequent requests. No second formula engine or cached-value permission fallback. Original SPN-078 acceptance, split 2026-10-21.
- Complete the existing account history and scoped offline-preparation integration deferred by SPN-091. Keep public and automatic Space history/capture/retention unavailable, preserving every existing checkpoint, until their exact Space/child scope and current account access are implemented and verified. Keep unsupported routes refused when considering ordinary activation; no bulk Space offline download or automatic Space Trash expiry is introduced. Routine decision #137, 2026-10-22; [Space review contract](../design/spaces.md#account-navigation-and-recovery-review).
- Preserve account-only identity isolation, generic guest presence, no guest recovery collection, uniform existing/future child permissions and exactly one Owner. Guests are View/Edit only; outer file administration remains owner-only until SPN-079. Owner, 2026-10-20–21; #117/#118/#126.
- Use the existing title/type-only preview pipeline for a purple Space social card; never publish child titles, content or source values. Follow [the approved Space design](../design/spaces.md) and use SPN-077 artwork. Owner, 2026-10-20; #117/#126.
- Prove IDs, content, ordering and formula bindings survive whole-Space folder moves/trash/restore, backups and SPN-064's implemented ownership-transfer path. Reuse its atomic all-owned-row handover and guest-capability invalidation; the complete activated Space integration still needs this ticket's proof. Preserve subtree recovery and bounded Operator resource use without adding content access. Original SPN-078 acceptance, split 2026-10-21; SPN-064 handoff, PR #142.
- Enable ordinary Space creation/navigation, guest links and integrations only after this access/recovery acceptance passes, including backups and owner transfer; unsupported Space/child routes refuse access. SPN-078/SPN-091 supply the guarded model and authenticated navigation/recovery. Grooming, 2026-10-21.
- Keep ordinary document lookup closed to Space rows; use one exact credential/root/child boundary through existing services and explicit root-only outer-file metadata/lifecycle composition. Re-estimated XL for cross-service authorization, recovery and adversarial verification. Routine decision #143, 2026-10-22; [implementation contract](../implementation/SPN-092.md).
- The guest/sharing design is approved in #126. For this active backlog run, independent subagent screenshot review covers all four personas and is recorded as **agent review only** under the owner's delegation in #139; other review and CI gates remain unchanged.

## Out of scope

- Delegated Admin (SPN-079), new formula syntax/engine, child grants/links, standalone-file conversion/extraction, bulk Space offline downloads and new export formats.

## Verify

- Browser View/Edit guests: navigate/deep-link, edit/reorder/nest/trash within their role, future additions, rotate/revoke/downgrade, unavailable external sources and no identity/location/recovery leakage. Review real Space social cards and owner-approved light/dark/phone stories/screenshots.
- Unit/API/integration: same-Space and independently authorized external formulas, unauthorized cross-Space/unit requests, async revocation across content/image/socket/MCP/export paths, backup/restore and SPN-064 owner transfer, whole-Space and nested trash/restore, stable IDs/order/bindings and unchanged non-Space behavior.
- Account history/offline integration: exact child and live-generation scope, checkpoint preservation and deliberate capture/retention activation, revocation/downgrade/trash/disabled-owner refusal across asynchronous reads and writes, and no guest recovery/history or unsupported offline capability. Preserve all deferred checkpoints until the supported path passes; record a separate retention decision before any automatic Space Trash expiry.
