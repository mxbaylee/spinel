# 👥 SPN-005 See and manage users and their passkeys

## Story

As the [Operator](../product/personas.md#operator), I want to see everyone on the instance and their passkey devices, and cut off access, so that a lost phone or a finished collaboration doesn't leave a door open.

## Scenarios

### Seeing who has access

Given I am the admin,
When I open the admin panel,
Then I see each user, their role, their passkey devices with last use, and open invites.

### Revoking access

Given a user or one of their devices,
When I remove it,
Then that device's sessions end at once, including open editor connections,
And removing a user disables the account: their documents and shares stay as they were.

### Admins only

Given a user who isn't an admin,
When they open the admin panel or its API,
Then they get a not-found response.

## Rules

- Reuse the existing auth/session and invite stores. Disable users without deleting auth_users rows (ownership currently cascades on deletion); enforce revocation on the next snapshot/image request and any live connection. Decided 2026-10-17 in #23.
- Removing a user never deletes or reassigns their documents, and never gives the admin access to them. Admins can't open documents (SPN-003). Grooming, 2026-10-17; weighs the removed person's privacy and their Collaborators' access over a tidy user list.

## Out of scope

- Server health numbers (SPN-032).

## Verify

- Unit tests for revocation ending sessions and preserving document ownership.
- Playwright: revoke a second user's device while they have a document open.
