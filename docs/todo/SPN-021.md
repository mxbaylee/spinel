# 🗑️ SPN-021 Undo deletes from the trash for 30 days

## Story

As the [Owner](../product/personas.md#owner), I want deleted documents and folders to wait in the trash for 30 days, so that a slip of the finger never loses anything.

## Scenarios

### Deleting and restoring

Given a document or folder,
When I delete it,
Then it moves to the trash with its contents and leaves everyone's views,
And restoring it puts it back where it was, or at the top level if that folder is gone.

### Permanent purge

Given an item has been in the trash 30 days,
When the daily cleanup runs,
Then it and its history are removed for good.

### Who can delete

Given a document shared with me,
When I try to delete it,
Then I can only remove it from my view; only the owner can trash it.

## Rules

- 30 days. PRD §2.1.
- "Empty trash" asks for confirmation and names how many items it removes.

## Out of scope

- Backups of purged data (SPN-030).

## Verify

- Unit tests for restore paths and the purge job with a fake clock.
- Playwright: delete a folder, restore it, check its contents.
