# 🗑️ SPN-021 Undo deletes from the trash for 30 days

## Story

As the [Owner](../product/personas.md#owner), I want deleted documents and folders to wait in the trash for 30 days, so that a slip of the finger never loses anything.

## Scenarios

### Deleting and restoring

Given a document or folder,
When I delete it,
Then it moves to the trash with its contents and leaves everyone's views,
And restoring it puts it back where it was, or at the top level if that folder is gone.

### Permanent purge

Given an item has been in the trash 30 days,
When the daily cleanup runs,
Then it and its history are removed for good.

### Who can delete

Given a document shared with me,
When I try to delete it,
Then I cannot trash it; only the owner can,
And removing a personal placement from my view is delivered with SPN-018.

## Rules

- Soft trash preserves native snapshots, images and history until permanent purge; do not trigger existing snapshot foreign-key cascades when trashing. Decided 2026-10-17 in #23.
- Trash active subtrees atomically, preserving earlier independent trash batches. Restore a selected batch/subtree to its active original parent or root without resurrecting earlier deletions. Decided 2026-10-17 in #30.
- Reject regular access to trashed content for everyone; preserve grants and resume current permissions on restore. Decided 2026-10-17 in #30.
- Purge after 30 elapsed UTC days; run at startup and every 24 hours, deleting children before parents. PRD §2.1; #30, 2026-10-17.
- Trash lists all retained items. "Empty trash" confirms the exact item count and refuses if that set changes before submission. Decided 2026-10-17 in #30.

## Out of scope

- Backups of purged data (SPN-030); collaborator placement removal (SPN-018, #30).

## Verify

- Unit tests for restore paths and the purge job with a fake clock.
- Playwright: delete a folder, restore it, check its contents.
