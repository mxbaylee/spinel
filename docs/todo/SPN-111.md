# ⏸️ SPN-111 Pause edits for supervised workspace recovery

## Story

As the [Operator](../product/personas.md#operator), I want a controlled maintenance pause, so that recovery can stop and restart the app without losing track of accepted work or current authority.

## Scenarios

### Pause and resume all writers

Given active editors, background jobs and a scheduled maintenance notice,
When an authorized Operator pauses edits,
Then the server gates every write path, drains accepted work to a bounded deadline and fences retries,
And clients see authoritative maintenance and pending-work status until an authorized safe resume.

### Recover control after a process interruption

Given an interrupted maintenance transition,
When the application and supervisor restart,
Then a durable external journal identifies the active generation and incomplete operation,
And unknown state stays in maintenance until verified recovery.

## Rules

- Follow [supervised maintenance](../implementation/SPN-063-backup-options.md#assisted-restore-is-a-supervised-maintenance-operation) and [notices](../implementation/SPN-063-backup-options.md#pause-resume-and-scheduled-notices), owner chat 2026-10-27; originating PR #205.
- Keep the narrow recovery journal, maintenance gate and operation authorization outside both workspace and disposable diagnostics. No unrestricted Docker socket or arbitrary shell/path API.
- Explicit current Operator authority, CSRF/origin protection, operation IDs and expiry. Restored credentials cannot authorize their own activation.
- Notices store an absolute instant and IANA zone, including DST/missed-job policy. A banner or scheduled pause does not authorize destructive restore.
- Bound drain/stop/start and verify all DB handles/WAL owners are closed. Retain independent proxy maintenance and CLI/cold-host control when application auth is unavailable.
- Prove real disposable process/container shutdown and kill/restart transitions locally, with scoped cleanup and declared resource budgets. Provider/actual-host acceptance stays before launch.
- [PR #206](https://github.com/mxbaylee/spinel/pull/206) remains in progress: its refreshed candidate records focused regression and supervised browser/visual evidence, while final-head hosted CI/Caddy checks and independent review are still required. Preserve its work and distinguish process-crash evidence from target-host/power-loss proof. [Grooming checkpoint](../implementation/grooming-2026-10-27.md#concurrent-work), 2026-10-27.
- Operator gains predictable control; Owner and Collaborator see acknowledgment/pending-work consequences; Link guest gets minimal status and retains scope/revocation. Independent visual review applies.

## Out of scope

- Replacing the live dataset from a chosen backup (SPN-112), unattended scheduled restoration, email/SMS notices.

## Verify

- Real HTTP/sync/job writers obey pause and drain; old/offline retries are fenced; authority expires safely; notice/cancel/DST behavior; real crash reconciliation and proxy visibility.
- Current light/dark/phone states and independent review; scoped supervisor operations cannot execute arbitrary commands or touch unrelated files.
