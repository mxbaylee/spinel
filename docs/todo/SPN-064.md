# 🤝 SPN-064 Reactivate accounts and hand over a departed person's documents

## Story

As the [Owner](../product/personas.md#owner), I want documents a departed Collaborator created to be handed to me, and a mistakenly disabled account to come back, so that shared work is never stranded with an account nobody can use. The [Operator](../product/personas.md#operator) does this without seeing any document.

## Scenarios

### Reactivating

Given a disabled account,
When the Operator chooses **Reactivate** and confirms,
Then the person can be sent a recovery invite, and their documents, grants and history are as they were.

### Handing over documents

Given a disabled account owns documents and folders,
When the Operator chooses **Transfer ownership** to an active everyday account and confirms,
Then that account owns them, existing grants keep working, and guest links that died with the old owner can be turned on again by the new one.

### The new owner's view

Given documents were transferred to me,
When I open the dashboard,
Then they are in a folder named after the previous owner, and their history and trash state are kept.

## Rules

- Disable stays the only way to remove someone, and still never deletes rows (#23). Reactivation never restores old passkeys or sessions; recovery invites still do that (invitations.md).
- The Operator sees only counts (items, folders, links), never names or contents, at every step.
- Transfer is one transaction: ownership, the containing folder, grants and live editor access are rechecked together. The old owner keeps no access.
- Never transfer to an admin, to the same account, or to a disabled account.

## Out of scope

- Members transferring ownership themselves. Deleting accounts.

## Verify

- Unit: reactivate, transfer, grants preserved, admin and disabled targets refused, and a rollback on failure.
- Playwright: disable a Collaborator, transfer their documents to the Owner, open one, and reactivate the Collaborator through a recovery invite.
