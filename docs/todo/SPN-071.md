# 🧺 SPN-071 Leave shares and see where my access comes from

## Story

As a [Collaborator](../product/personas.md#collaborator), I want to hide shares I'm done with, see which folder gives me access, and pick up a new role without reloading, so that Shared with me stays short and I know why I can or can't edit.

## Scenarios

### Hiding a share

Given a document shared with me that I no longer need,
When I choose **Hide** from its row's More menu,
Then it leaves Shared with me, search and my pins, and **Show hidden** brings it back,
And the owner's grant and my access don't change.

### Where access comes from

Given I can edit a Doc because its folder "Budgets" is shared with me,
When I open the Doc's details,
Then it says "Editor through Budgets".

### A role upgrade

Given I have a document open as a viewer,
When the owner makes me an editor,
Then within a few seconds the editor becomes editable, without a reload and without losing my place.

## Rules

- Hiding is personal, like filing: it never changes owner, parent or grants (docs/sharing.md, #30).
- "Through <folder>" needs the resolver to report the granting ancestor (docs/design/sharing.md). Name it only when I can open that folder.
- Upgrades reuse the collaboration access scan (docs/collaboration.md); downgrades already lock on the next request.

- When Spaces exist, show both outer folder inheritance and the Space boundary without exposing inaccessible ancestors. Hiding/filing a Space operates on the outer file; it never creates independent child sharing or personal child placement (SPN-078, #116).

## Out of scope

- Declining a share on the owner's side. Notifications.

## Verify

- Unit: hide, show, search and pins filtering, and inherited-source reporting with an inaccessible ancestor.
- Playwright: hide and restore a share; upgrade a viewer while the editor is open, and type.
