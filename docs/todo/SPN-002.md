# 🔑 SPN-002 Sign in with a passkey

## Story

As the [Owner](../product/personas.md#owner), I want to sign in with a passkey and no password, so that my documents are protected without anything to remember or leak.

## Scenarios

### Signing in

Given an account with a registered passkey,
When I choose "Sign in" and confirm with my device,
Then I land on my dashboard with a session that survives a page reload.

### Adding another device

Given I am signed in,
When I add a passkey from my phone,
Then both devices can sign me in and both appear in my account settings.

### Signed-out access

Given no session,
When I open any page or API route other than sign-in, invite and share links,
Then I am sent to sign-in and no document data is returned.

## Rules

- WebAuthn through `@simplewebauthn/server` and `@simplewebauthn/browser`. PRD §4.
- Sessions are server-side rows in SQLite, sent as an HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Strict cookie, so they can be revoked. Chosen over JWTs in grooming, 2026-10-17.
- No password fallback. Recovery is the Operator issuing a new invite (SPN-004).

## Out of scope

- Creating accounts: first admin (SPN-003) and invites (SPN-004).

## Verify

- Unit tests for registration and assertion verification, counter checks and session expiry.
- Playwright with a virtual authenticator: register, sign out, sign in, add a second device.
