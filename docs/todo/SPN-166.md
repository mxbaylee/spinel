# 🎲 SPN-166 Stop the Playwright smoke jobs from flaking

## Story

As the [Operator](../product/personas.md#operator), I want the Playwright smoke jobs to pass or fail for real reasons, so that a green gate means the product works and a flake does not cost a re-run.

## Scenarios

### One transient failure does not decide the gate

Given a smoke project runs a case that has flaked (the examples below),
When it fails on a first attempt and passes on retry,
Then the cause is identified and either fixed or the case fails with a clear diagnostic, rather than passing on retry and still failing the job.

## Rules

- Follow [sustainable testing](../testing.md); keep the Verify plan and progress evidence current. [Policy](../implementation/SPN-115.md#guidance-for-every-open-ticket-and-progress-checkpoint), owner 2026-11-01.
- Observed on [PR 289](https://github.com/mxbaylee/spinel/pull/289), CI run 43789748511, attempt 1: `offline-recovery.spec.ts:342` ("Space child sheet: trashed live document yields one owned copy and leaves original untouched") hit `ECONNRESET` on `POST /api/items/<id>/collaboration` in `copySnapshot` and passed on retry. 99 passed, 1 flaky, and the job still exited 1. Re-running the failed jobs passed (attempt 2). The cause is **not diagnosed**: it could be the test server dropping a connection under load, a test setup race, or a product bug in that endpoint. Find out which before changing any test.
- Impact raised from M to L (agent decision, 2026-11-17, owner to confirm): the required `Playwright smoke` gate blocks every merge, and three cases in three projects have now cost reruns of about 20 minutes each on two PRs, which blocks the Operator's main job of landing tickets.
- More examples, all passing on retry and failing the job, none diagnosed:
  - [PR 294](https://github.com/mxbaylee/spinel/pull/294), CI run 43992274196, attempts 1 and 2: `restore.spec.ts:95` ("sheet preview is read only and restore preserves the state seen by both editors") in `development 2/2`, position 8 of 82, failed its first attempt both times and passed on retry. The preview popup never showed the heading "Preview — Restore example" in 5 s and showed the history page instead. It passed first try on main's run 43967042239 in the same position and passed locally twice. PR 294 changes nothing near it. **Checkpoint 2 (most likely cause, unproven until CI shows it):** the heading shows the item's name only once `data` loads (`web/VersionPreview.tsx:142`), and the Restore button is enabled by the same `ready` state (`:131`), the load the test gives 30 s. The check ahead of it had Playwright's default 5 s, an unplanned shorter deadline; the load takes 2.3 to 2.9 s locally (3 runs) and may have exceeded 5 s on the slow CI runner (no CI load time was measured). Fixed in `e2e/restore.spec.ts` by waiting for readiness under its one 30 s budget first (worst case stays 35 s, as before), and the time to ready is recorded as a `preview-ready-ms` annotation. The case still fails when the preview does not load. Owner to confirm this is not a relaxed budget.
  - Same run, attempt 1: `offline-upgrade.spec.ts:675` ("interrupted emitted updates preserve exact native work, a live old client and cold offline startup") in the `offline-upgrade` project. The emitted worker stayed `installed` and never activated for 120 s; it passed on retry and on attempt 2. **Checkpoint 3 (cause narrowed, not fixed):** three runs (43992274196, 42662177748, 42661952543) show the same trace: the new worker reaches `installed` (waiting) within 21 to 518 ms, `skipWaiting()` in its install handler never takes effect, and the old worker stays active until the 120 s timeout. Per the service worker spec a waiting worker activates only when the active one has no in-flight extendable event (a hypothesis here, not observed), and the old worker's `message` handler holds one through the shared runtime Web Lock for up to 180 s, so a lock or message that does not drain is the leading suspect. The 13 other failures of the SPN-054 eager-client case all came from branch `codex/spn-146-mobile-flush` (attribution from the run's branch name, not re-checked per run) and are treated as that branch's own failures. Diagnostic added: the timeout error now includes `navigator.locks.query()` (held and pending lock names, modes and client ids). Names keep only the prefix before `:`, clients are numbered, and the query is capped at 2 s (the 120 s failure can arrive up to 2 s later). It cannot run locally here (it needs the immutable legacy checkout).
- Do not weaken the gate. No exclusions, added retries, relaxed assertions or budgets; a flaky pass must keep failing the job unless the cause is fixed. The `Playwright smoke` aggregate stays required.
- 📊 **Workflow history (2026-11-17).** The last 300 `ci.yml` runs (2026-11-06 to 2026-11-17) had 62 runs with a failed Playwright smoke job attempt. 15 distinct cases failed their first attempt and passed on retry, 23 case-run hits in all. Counts are lower bounds: job logs for multi-attempt runs can return the passing attempt. Most frequent:
  - `collaboration.spec.ts`: "revoking one login preserves another login's queued edit…" in 3 runs (`production`, plus 1 in `development`) and "two document editors merge text and navigate to a collaborator selection" in 5 job-attempts (3 `development`, 2 `production`). **Checkpoint 4 (no code change):** "revoking one login" flaked on 2026-11-07, 11-08, 11-14 and 11-15 (one `main` run) because navigation can close the revoked socket without any Playwright event; that cause was diagnosed and fixed by SPN-149 and SPN-161 (merged 2026-11-17), and 3 CI runs since have been green, too few to call it closed, so watch for a recurrence. For "two document editors", 8 of the 9 job-attempts were one branch (`codex/spn-146-mobile-flush`, copied-text mismatch) and are that branch's own failures; the ninth (run 42938326822) was the strict one-second delivery budget missed by 5 ms (1005 ms). That budget is the product's contract, so it stays unchanged and its message already reports both timings.
  - 2 runs each: the `restore.spec.ts` preview case and two `offline-upgrade.spec.ts` cases ("interrupted emitted updates…", "the actual SPN-054 eager client…", which also failed outright 13 times). 1 run each: eight others, including `space-guests.spec.ts:892`, `portable.spec.ts:10`, `admin.spec.ts`, `maintenance.spec.ts`, `offline.spec.ts`, `offline-editing.spec.ts`, `offline-recovery.spec.ts` and `supervised-restore.spec.ts`.
- 🔌 **`ECONNRESET` recurs on the test's own HTTP client.** Three more first attempts, all in `development 2/2`, hit `apiRequestContext … read ECONNRESET`: `restore.spec.ts` (the same preview case, line numbers differ between checkouts) and `portable.spec.ts:10` (`GET /api/items/<id>/sheet`) and `space-guests.spec.ts:892` (a Space item export `GET`). The endpoint varies, so it is not specific to the collaboration endpoint. PR 289's own log is not recoverable (it returns the passing attempt). A throwaway repro (not committed) of the default Node 5 s keep-alive against a Playwright request context did not reset at 4.9 to 6 s idle, so that cause was not reproduced in that one setup. Checkpoint 1 diagnostic: `e2e/reset-diagnostics-reporter.ts` (CI reporter, observe-only) logs one content-free `econnreset-diagnostic` JSON line per reset failure, with the call (`GET /api/items/<id>/sheet`) and the owned server's `server-<port>.json` state (pid, exited, signal, byte counts, evidence age), and saves `playwright-report/reset-diagnostics-<project>.json`. The next CI reset names its cause: a dead or restarted process, or a live server dropping one connection.
- 🧩 **One ticket, several causes (owner decision, chat 2026-11-17).** The examples do not share one cause, and the owner chose to fix them in this one ticket rather than split it. Effort raised from M to L accordingly. Work one checkpoint per cause, and record each cause, fix and no-flake evidence here: (1) `apiRequestContext` `ECONNRESET`, (2) the `restore.spec.ts` preview popup, (3) the `offline-upgrade.spec.ts` worker never activating, (4) the `collaboration.spec.ts` flakes. Re-estimate at the first checkpoint, and if a cause turns out much larger than the rest, split only that cause with its full remaining estimate.

## Out of scope

- Playwright cases that have not flaked in recorded history. The one-off flakes listed above count as in scope only when they share a cause with a fixed case.

## Verify

- Contract/negative case: `scripts/ci/reset-diagnostics.test.ts` covers the reset diagnostic (privacy, cap, missing evidence, exited server); the restore wait and lock snapshot are covered by CI runs only; suite/layer/fixture: the existing project and fixture of each example (`sync-production`, `development`, `offline-upgrade`).
- Fixture readiness/cleanup: the existing offline-recovery, restore preview and offline-upgrade fixtures and server lifecycle.
- Environment and source/shared dependencies: `e2e/offline-recovery.spec.ts`, `e2e/restore.spec.ts`, `e2e/offline-upgrade.spec.ts`, the collaboration endpoint, the CI plan for the affected jobs.
- Evidence: repeated runs of each affected project with no flake, linked by run id, and the workflow history count from before.
