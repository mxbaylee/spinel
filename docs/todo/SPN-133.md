# ⏱️ SPN-133 Keep native Doc flushes within their established budgets

## Story

As the [Owner](../product/personas.md#owner), I want native Doc editing to stay responsive through capacity and idle flushes, so that typing and saving do not stall.

## Scenarios

### Both flush paths meet their existing budgets

Given the established desktop and throttled-phone native typing workloads,
When capacity and idle flushes run,
Then their first-attempt measurements meet the existing budgets,
And authored content, browser-local drain, peer convergence and complete Undo/Redo remain correct.

## Rules

- Follow [sustainable testing](../testing.md); keep the Verify plan and progress evidence current. [Policy](../implementation/SPN-115.md#guidance-for-every-open-ticket-and-progress-checkpoint), owner 2026-11-01.
- Diagnose the retained desktop idle-flush and main mobile maximum failures before selecting a repair. Preserve their different source/runner/sample identities and do not infer GC, data loss or a feedback regression from timing alone. [Origin, scope and capability checkpoint](../implementation/SPN-133.md#native-flush-tail-failures), agent judgment 2026-11-02, originating in SPN-098 / PR #227 and the SPN-071 main push.
- Keep desktop p95 16 ms / maximum 50 ms and mobile p95 50 ms / maximum 100 ms, existing input/drain limits, fresh-profile first attempts, fail-on-flaky behavior and exact content/access assertions unchanged. No forced GC, assertion retries, workload shortening, baseline substitution or diagnostic-profile acceptance.
- Use the smallest evidence-backed native implementation or observation-boundary repair. A diagnosis that proves measurement error must preserve equivalent user-visible latency coverage; a green unrelated run is not a disposition for either failure.
- Initial L covers bounded attribution and a coherent repair with differential correctness and hosted verification. Reassess to XL before sustained algorithmic or architectural work; split a separable outcome explicitly without losing either original failure's obligation.
- SPN-132 fixed a specific supported paragraph-marker composition path; it did not establish that every native timing tail is solved. Keep its fallback correctness and licensing boundaries intact. Any replacement package follows the repository's `lib/<name>/` contract.

## Out of scope

- Feedback submission/export features, SPN-120's finite fixture inventory, SPN-131's dependency setup, or SPN-134's incomplete historical callback run.
- Weakening merge gates or treating preserved content as permission to ignore failed latency acceptance.

Keep diagnostic instrumentation separate from unprofiled acceptance.
- Demonstrate the selected repair against the identified old path or a meaningful negative control at the cheapest layer. Check exact operation composition, formatting/paragraph structure, history and collaboration when changed; preserve unsupported-input fallback behavior.
- Run the unchanged desktop/mobile first-attempt candidate owners and applicable historical comparisons on the final source. Include observed idle and capacity coverage; if a required path is not observed, record that gap and use a predeclared targeted proof rather than claiming a pass from null measurements.
- Preserve every original/diagnostic/acceptance attempt, exact source/base/tested SHA, runtime/browser/runner, raw metrics/profiles and content/Undo/Redo/access results. Recheck source/shared-helper/patch routing, cleanup task-owned resources and obey the local watchdog. Required CI and independent reviews remain mandatory.
