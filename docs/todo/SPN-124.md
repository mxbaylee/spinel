# ⏱️ SPN-124 Let restore work outlast the passkey check

## Story

As the [Operator](../product/personas.md#operator), I want a large restore to finish after one fresh passkey check, so that a slow upload or activation does not fail or leave the workspace closed.

## Scenarios

### A multi-GiB upload or activation takes longer than five minutes

Given a fresh Operator passkey check at create and again at confirm,
When upload, verification, reconciliation or supervised activation runs past five minutes,
Then the operation continues within its own bounded lifetime while that session and account stay current,
And revocation, sign-out, target change or the operation's own deadline still stops it before the next durable cutover.

## Rules

- Split by SPN-112's merge review, 2026-10-30: [disposition](../implementation/SPN-112.md#merge-review-dispositions). SPN-112 reports an expired passkey window distinctly and documents the limit; it does not change the window.
- Today the create proof bounds preparation (`restore-registry.ts` signals and `assertRestoreAuthority`) and the confirm proof bounds the supervisor's preparation, release and original recovery (`intent.authority.expiresAt`, `AUTHORITY_MS`). Fresh proof stays required at create and confirm; only the work's duration separates from it.
- Keep the journal fences: an intent's operation deadline is durable, hashed with the intent and checked at every cutover the way `authority.expiresAt` is today. A shorter-lived authority cannot be renewed by a later passkey check.
- Persona weighing: the Operator needs large restores to complete; the Owner and Collaborators need the workspace closed no longer than necessary; nobody gains from a stolen session continuing, so current-session and current-account checks remain.

## Out of scope

- Resumable uploads, background restores without an open Admin page, changing the 5 GiB or 8 GiB limits.

## Verify

- Unit and supervisor tests that cross the old five-minute boundary in preparation, activation and original recovery, with revocation and deadline cases at each cutover.
- Measured end-to-end timing at a realistic database and archive size on the 1 GiB / 0.75 CPU profile, recorded in the PR.
