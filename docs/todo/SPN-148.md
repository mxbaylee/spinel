# 🖱️ SPN-148 Diagnose recurring native Safari click delivery

## Story

As the [Operator](../product/personas.md#operator), I want native Safari checks to explain missing input, so that updates can be qualified without uncertain reruns.

## Scenarios

### A missing native click has a bounded disposition

Given the retained Safari failure after the document loaded,
When its identity, target, posting and page-observation evidence is investigated,
Then a demonstrated fixture defect is repaired and verified, or the predeclared sample ends with an explicit inconclusive/not-reproduced disposition,
And a missing click still fails within its original bound without claiming the native journey passed.

## Rules

- Owner approved this M, pre-launch outcome during grooming, chat 2026-11-12. Preserve the [registration, first failure and persona decision](../implementation/SPN-148.md#-registration-and-scope).
- Follow [sustainable testing](../testing.md). Inspect retained evidence before declaring one falsifiable hypothesis, exact source/browser/OS, a small fixed fresh-profile sample, resource bounds and stop condition. Reassess before broader OS/browser integration or a newly demonstrated product defect.
- Keep real trusted input, positive process/target identity, current deadlines and bounded owned cleanup. No synthetic replacement click, arbitrary sleep, increased timeout or failed-assertion retry.
- Preserve complete affected native content, images, reconnect, storage-loss and access acceptance. Later green runs do not establish the original cause, and an inconclusive diagnosis does not waive a failed current-head gate.
- Keep SPN-137's completed outcome and historical evidence intact. This recurrence is distinct from SPN-106's viewer warning, SPN-120's finite historical inventory, SPN-145's worker activation and SPN-090's real phones.

## Out of scope

- Replacing the native automation framework, weakening acceptance, or a general Safari/OS investigation without a new scope checkpoint.

## Verify

- Extend `scripts/ci/desktop-pointer.test.ts` for the demonstrated observation/driver boundary; include successful delivery and wrong/missing target, failed posting or absent observation controls as applicable.
- Reuse `scripts/ci/desktop-pointer.ts`, `desktop-browser.ts`, `desktop-offline.ts` and their owned fixtures. Observe identity/readiness explicitly; preserve the first failure alongside cleanup errors without touching other profiles.
- Focused tests own deterministic controls; the maintained native macOS Safari workflow owns trusted-input and full offline/reconnect/storage-loss acceptance. Track driver/fixture, browser-version, application and workflow dependencies in existing routing.
- Retain source/tested SHA, browser/OS, every attempt, original evidence limits and the declared sample's disposition. Stop when exhausted; no loop until green. Both independent reviews and all selected current-head checks remain required.
