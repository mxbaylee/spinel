# 🗺️ SPN-091 Navigate and recover a Space's pages

## Story

As the [Owner](../product/personas.md#owner), I want to open related Docs and Sheets from one Space and recover pages I trash, so that organizing a project is easy to follow and undo. Signed-in [Collaborators](../product/personas.md#collaborator) use the same navigation within their granted role.

## Scenarios

### A persistent, accessible content tree

Given I create a Space in an existing folder and open its empty state,
When I add Docs, Sheets and folders, edit content, rename, reorder and nest them,
Then the tree persists across reloads, keyboard/touch controls offer alternatives to dragging, and remembered selection, Back/Forward and stable deep links restore my place.

### Recover a deleted subtree

Given an account editor trashes a page, Sheet or folder with children,
When they open the Space Trash collection and restore it,
Then its contents, IDs, order and formula bindings recover together at the readable original location, or the Space root with an explanation if that parent is unavailable; permitted permanent deletion clearly names the affected subtree.

## Rules

- Follow [Spaces and file roles](../design/spaces.md) and the SPN-047 approved composition: one internal sidebar replaces outer navigation, a Pages drawer serves phones, and pages have separate open/expand controls plus visible More actions. Use SPN-077 artwork. Owner, 2026-10-20; #116/#126.
- Use SPN-078's inherited access and durable tree; View never mutates, Edit manages internal content/tree and restores, and only Owner permanently deletes under existing rules. Outer sharing/rename/move/trash stays owner-only until SPN-079. Owner, 2026-10-20–21; #118/#126.
- Trash is a collection, using the My files Trash pattern with type/identity, readable original location, deletion/recovery information and permitted Restore/Delete controls. Preserve subtrees and inaccessible-location privacy; confirm permanent deletion and leave content intact on cancel. Owner, 2026-10-21; #126.
- Wire real editor, tree and recovery persistence for review/testing; keep ordinary Space creation/navigation and guest links disabled until SPN-092 verifies the remaining integration and recovery boundaries, including backups and owner transfer. Original SPN-078 scope, split 2026-10-21.
- Reject unimplemented capabilities and check revocation, downgrade, trash and disabled owners in open editors and after async work. Original SPN-078 access requirements, carried forward 2026-10-21.

## Out of scope

- Guest navigation and cross-feature acceptance (SPN-092), delegated Admin (SPN-079), child-specific grants, standalone-file conversion/extraction, bulk offline downloads or new export formats.

## Verify

- Browser owner/editor/viewer: create, empty state, add/edit both document types, folders/page children, rename, reorder/nest without drag, reload/deep links and Back/Forward; unsupported cycles/moves and unauthorized actions fail safely.
- Persisted trash/restore: complete subtrees, missing original parent, private locations, bindings and IDs; permitted deletion, confirmation/cancel, revoked/read-only/disabled access and offline-unavailable management. Storybook and owner-approved light/dark/phone screenshots cover navigation and the recovery collection.
