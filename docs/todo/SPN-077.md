# 🚚 SPN-077 Cut and paste cells to another sheet while someone else is editing

## Story

As a [Collaborator](../product/personas.md#collaborator), I want to cut cells from one sheet and paste them on another while the [Owner](../product/personas.md#owner) is editing either sheet, so that I can reorganise a workbook without waiting for them.

## Scenarios

### A cut while the other sheet changes

Given two people in one workbook,
When one cuts B2:C3 from Sheet1 to Sheet2 while the other inserts a row at the top of Sheet2,
Then both screens show the cells one row lower on Sheet2 and gone from Sheet1.

### A cut while the source cells change

Given two people in one workbook,
When one cuts B2 to another sheet while the other types into B2,
Then both screens show the same value at the destination, and B2 empty.

## Rules

- SPN-056 rejects a range move whose source and target sheets differ, because each of its transforms addresses one sheet. SPN-059 hides cut-and-paste to another sheet until this lands. SPN-056 review, 2026-10-19.
- Add the transform to `lib/sync-transform/`, verified against headless Univer and in the combined fuzz, as SPN-056 does.

## Out of scope

- Moving cells to another workbook.

## Verify

- Unit: a cross-sheet move against every sheet family on either sheet, including removing either sheet. The combined fuzz sends cross-sheet moves.
