# 📐 SPN-159 Observe completed native drawing resizes

## Story

As the [Operator](../product/personas.md#operator), I want the native drawing check to observe each completed editor resize, so that it verifies real drawing behavior and identifies a failed transition without uncertain reruns.

## Scenarios

### Narrowing and restoring complete the intended native transitions

Given the native fixture has inserted and selected its synthetic image,
When the editor narrows and then returns to its original width,
Then each intended host/native geometry transition and required drawing-control refresh is observed,
And unrelated resize activity cannot make missing narrow or restore work pass.

### Selection and resize leave the authored pending work intact

Given image insertion has produced the expected native pending edit,
When the image and neighboring text are selected and the editor resizes,
Then the pending authored work remains unchanged,
And UI-only selection operations never enter the pending mutation queue.

## Rules

- Registration only; the retained desktop-light failure is `expected 2 to be greater than 2`, with no exact assertion/phase identity. The final full-width geometry does not establish its cause. [First failure and limits](../implementation/SPN-159.md#-retained-failure-and-source-limits), agent judgment, 2026-11-13.
- Complete **M** includes a predeclared bounded source/native experiment, phase/geometry diagnostics, demonstrated readiness repair, meaningful negative controls, native insertion/selection/control refresh, pending-mutation safety, integration and all existing selected Storybook variants. [Scope and personas](../implementation/SPN-159.md#-complete-outcome-and-persona-decision). Reassess before a broader native product/rendering/persistence repair.
- Investigate the global-counter readiness hypothesis; do not label it the historical diagnosis or a product defect. Identify the actual transition and correct its demonstrated boundary, preserving original deadlines, assertions, privacy and cleanup. No arbitrary sleep, blanket retry, raised budget, weakened comparison or unchanged rerun until green.
- Reuse `web/SyncNative.stories.tsx` and the existing screenshot/native geometry helpers. Prove missing narrow and restore transitions cannot pass, including unrelated pending resize activity where relevant. Retain native image insertion, text/image selection, control refresh, unchanged pending count/IDs, absent `doc.operation.clear-drawing-transformer` mutation, no native failures and no alerts.
- This is distinct from SPN-120's finite historical fixture inventory and SPN-087's obsolete CSS cleanup. Both retain their original scope; registering this prerequisite does not waive a failed gate. Follow [sustainable testing](../testing.md).
