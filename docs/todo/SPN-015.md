# 🔒 SPN-015 Share a document or folder with specific people

## Story

As the [Owner](../product/personas.md#owner), I want to share a document or a whole folder with chosen people as viewers or editors, so that we can work together while everything else stays private.

## Scenarios

### Private by default

Given a new document or folder,
When anyone other than me looks for it,
Then it doesn't exist for them.

### Sharing a folder

Given a folder I share with a Collaborator as editor,
When they open it,
Then they can edit everything in it, including documents added later,
And they can't share it onward or delete it.

### Changing my mind

Given something I shared,
When I change someone to viewer or remove them,
Then it takes effect on their next action, including open editors.

## Rules

- Apply one permission resolver to metadata, snapshot reads/writes and document image reads/uploads when SPN-008 lands. Preserve admin exclusion and revision-based stale-write rejection. Decided 2026-10-17 in #23.
- Folder sharing replaces Team Spaces. Owner, chat 2026-10-17.
- The most generous grant wins when a document is shared both directly and through a folder. Grooming, 2026-10-17.

## Out of scope

- Link sharing (SPN-016), "Shared with me" (SPN-018), checks on live connections (SPN-017).

## Verify

- Unit tests for the permission resolver: direct, inherited, revoked, mixed.
- Playwright: share a folder, add a document to it, see the Collaborator get it.
