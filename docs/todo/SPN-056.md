# 🧬 SPN-056 Independently verify integrated Sheet and Doc sync before activation

## Story

As a [Collaborator](../product/personas.md#collaborator), I want a second independent review to prove that simultaneous edits keep their intended content through reconnect and reopen, so that Spinel's own sync is safe before it becomes the editing path.

## Scenarios

### Review the integrated first pass

Given SPN-104's transform library and SPN-057/SPN-058's server and client are merged behind the disabled flag,
When a fresh agent reviews the complete Sheet and Doc sync path and exercises real editor commands through it,
Then it records an independent score against the documented rubric, and any blocking findings are repaired and independently re-reviewed until the original no-loss and convergence acceptance is demonstrated,
And SPN-059 cannot activate the replacement until this review passes.

### Meaning survives reconnect and reopen

Given two editors have overlapping pending edits, including default-name sheet additions or renames and formulas referring to them,
When the clients reconnect, receive acknowledgments and reopen their saved native snapshots,
Then both preserve the same sheet identities, intended formula targets and supported edits, or follow the documented refusal/resync contract without claiming rejected edits were saved.
