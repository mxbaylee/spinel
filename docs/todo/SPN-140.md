# 🧭 SPN-140 Resolve native Safari automation after offline restart

## Story

As the [Operator](../product/personas.md#operator), I want native Safari checks to address the verified browser after restart, so that maintenance results prove real offline behavior.

## Scenarios

### A restarted browser remains positively identified through dispatch

Given an ordinary Safari profile restarted with transport unavailable,
When the harness establishes its exact identity and performs its retained-storage probe,
Then the intended live process receives each action once,
And missing, changed or denied identities fail with the original cause and bounded cleanup.

## Rules

- Follow [sustainable testing](../testing.md); keep the Verify plan and progress evidence current. [Policy](../implementation/SPN-115.md#guidance-for-every-open-ticket-and-progress-checkpoint), owner 2026-11-01.
- Preserve the [original failure, L scope and authority boundaries](../implementation/SPN-140.md#registration-and-scope), agent judgment 2026-11-04 after the owner requested continued SPN-120 repair.
- Integrate accepted SPN-141 before deliberate validation, as recorded in the [owner-requested draft checkpoint](../implementation/SPN-142.md#dependency-and-scope-accounting); its SPN-142 blocker is inherited without widening this constructor repair.
- Investigate the demonstrated JXA constructor failure after successful process validation; do not assume transient failure or retry safety. Preserve exact PID, bundle, executable, launch time, normal consent and existing time limits.
- No repeated dispatched JavaScript, authentication/storage mutations or failed assertions. Any bounded pre-dispatch preparation must prove no action was sent and preserve all attempts; no name/path fallback may silently choose another process.
- Keep complete same-profile sentinel retention, owned teardown and full native application acceptance. An independent storage-loss failure remains owned by SPN-141 and blocks integration.

## Out of scope

- SPN-120's finite historical audit, native storage-loss setup, product UI changes, broader automation framework replacement or owner-profile Safari experiments.

## Verify

- Extend existing desktop-safari-identity controls for the selected constructor/dispatch boundary; wrong/reused identity, exit, denial and a dispatched-action failure must stay fatal.
- Retain request mode, constructor/dispatch phases, identity and bounded owned cleanup; existing scripts/ci/desktop-*.ts routing selects native acceptance.
- Before publication, declare one complete first-attempt native workflow for the concrete repair. Require same-profile localStorage/IDB/cache/worker retention, both valid baseline helpers and full Safari application proof; preserve every failure, no unchanged rerun.
- Record source/base/tested identities, phase timings, raw first failures, useful screenshots, all required gates and two exact-source reviews. Controls alone do not prove native behavior.
