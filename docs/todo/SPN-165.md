# 🧮 SPN-165 Find why a phone Sheet shows no formula value

## Story

As an [Owner](../product/personas.md#owner), I want a formula cell to show the same value on my phone as on my computer, so that I can trust a Sheet on both. [Collaborators](../product/personas.md#collaborator) read the same Sheets.

## Scenarios

### A formula shows its value on the phone

Given a Sheet where A1:A4 hold 10, 15, 20 and 26 and B5 holds `=SUM(A:A)`,
When it is opened on the phone,
Then B5 shows 71, as it does on the computer.

## Rules

- Follow [sustainable testing](../testing.md); keep the Verify plan and progress evidence current. [Policy](../implementation/SPN-115.md#guidance-for-every-open-ticket-and-progress-checkpoint), owner 2026-11-01.

- Observed in the SPN-156 own-phone run, 2026-11-16 (owner): Pixel 10, Chrome 157.0.8103.78, installed app, build `ccc201ef` of [PR 289](https://github.com/mxbaylee/spinel/pull/289). B5 showed **71** on the computer and **no value** on the phone. Seen once and **not reproduced**; cause unknown, and the phone Sheet was also edited offline in that run.
- First reproduce: create the formula on the computer, open on the phone online, then also try after an offline edit and a reconnect. Separate three causes: the phone never recalculates, the value is not synced or persisted, or only the render is blank. Record which, with how it was observed.
- Not a regression claim: PR 289 changes replay and writer handoff, not formula calculation, but that was not tested.

## Out of scope

- Changing which functions are supported.

## Verify

- Contract/negative case: after the reproduction identifies the layer, a regression test at that layer asserts the formula value is present after load and after offline replay; suite/layer/fixture: to be named by the investigation.
- Fixture readiness/cleanup: a seeded Sheet with the SUM formula.
- Environment and source/shared dependencies: the Sheet calculation and sync paths the reproduction implicates.
- Evidence: the reproduction recorded before the fix, and the phone observation after.
