# 🪐 SPN-078 Keep related Docs and Sheets in one shared Space

## Story

As the [Owner](../product/personas.md#owner), I want one Space file containing related Docs and Sheets, so that I can organize and share a whole project. [Collaborators](../product/personas.md#collaborator) and [Link guests](../product/personas.md#link-guest) navigate the same contents within their granted role.

## Scenarios

### One file outside, a content tree inside

Given I create a Space in an existing folder,
When I open it,
Then its persistent internal sidebar lists its Docs and Sheets, with an empty state and ways to add either type,
And I can navigate, rename, reorder, nest and trash internal pages with keyboard/touch alternatives to drag-and-drop; Back/Forward and deep links restore my place.

### One access boundary

Given an account or guest has View or Edit access to a Space,
When they open any internal page, sheet, image or same-Space formula source,
Then the same effective Space role applies, including future additions, with no independent child grants or child guest links,
And revocation, downgrade, trash and disabled-owner checks also affect open editors and subsequent requests.

### Existing formulas and external sources

Given a Doc inside a Space uses scalar, range or multi-source formulas,
When it renders,
Then it reuses SPN-033's syntax, stable bindings, formatting and unavailable states; a source outside that Space still requires independent access,
And a Space guest link never grants access outside its Space or publishes formula values in social previews.

## Rules

- A Space is a single file in the existing folder view, not a replacement for folders or a separate top-level workspace. Opening it reveals an internal folder-like page/sheet tree. Owner, chat 2026-10-20; #116. Existing folder inheritance applies to the Space file; its effective role applies uniformly inside.
- Support Space-wide View/Edit guest links, including a purple Space social card through the existing title/type-only preview pipeline. No child titles, content or source values in that card. Owner, chat 2026-10-20; #117.
- One owner owns the Space and its contents. Edit manages internal content/tree like worksheet tabs; Viewer cannot mutate. Sharing, rename/move/trash of the outer Space remain owner-controlled until SPN-079 adds delegated file Admin. Owner inherits Admin, with exactly one Owner (#118; owner, chat 2026-10-20).
- Reuse current access, guest capability, collaboration, image, Formula and trash services. Check Space scope at every HTTP/socket/MCP/export boundary, including after async work; never create a second formula engine or cached-value permission fallback.
- SPN-047 first documents Space navigation, phone drawer, ownership/roles, guest sharing and recoverable internal trash in the playbook and mockup stories. Use SPN-077 artwork; retain account-only identity isolation and bounded resource use for the Operator.
- Preserve internal IDs, content, ordering and formula bindings through reload, whole-Space folder moves/trash/restore, backups and owner transfer. Nested navigation cannot form cycles. Unsupported moves of standalone files into/out of a Space are refused rather than retaining or inventing child grants.

## Out of scope

- Replacing folders; new formula syntax; page-specific grants; delegated Admin (SPN-079); standalone-file conversion/extraction; bulk Space offline downloads or a new export format.

## Verify

- Unit/API: uniform inherited role, future children, cycles, ownership, unauthorized child routes, cross-Space denial, same-Space/external formulas, async revocation and complete trash/restore.
- Browser: owner/editor/viewer and View/Edit guests; create/navigate/reorder/nest/trash/restore internal content, persisted deep links, guest rotation/revocation and privacy. Storybook plus light/dark/phone screenshots; inspect real Space social cards. Reassess splitting at the normal PR-size gate.
