# 💬 SPN-089 Check remaining dialogs for consistent title icons

## Story

As the [Owner](../product/personas.md#owner), I want dialogs to have a consistent visual cue beside their titles, so that actions remain recognizable across files, editors and settings. Collaborators, Operators and link guests get the same clarity within their permitted flows.

## Scenarios

### A coherent dialog heading

Given any product modal, including loading, error and destructive variants,
When it opens on desktop or phone,
Then an appropriate icon appears beside its visible title using shared sizing and alignment,
And the title, content, close control and actions remain readable without crowding.

### Preserve accessible meaning

Given keyboard or assistive-technology navigation,
When a dialog opens or closes,
Then its textual title remains its accessible name and focus handling is unchanged,
And decorative icons are hidden from assistive technology rather than announced twice.
