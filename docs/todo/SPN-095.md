# 🗑️ SPN-095 Expire Space Trash after 30 days

## Story

As the [Owner](../product/personas.md#owner), I want deleted Spaces and internal pages to use the same recovery window as ordinary Trash, so that recovery is predictable and deleted content does not accumulate indefinitely. The Operator gets bounded cleanup without seeing content.

## Scenarios

### A consistent recovery window
Given a deleted outer Space or internal subtree,
When 30 elapsed UTC days have passed since its deletion,
Then restoration is refused and bounded background cleanup permanently removes only expired content,
And the recovery UI shows the applicable expiry before then.

### Recovery and cleanup race
Given a subtree with earlier independent deletion batches and a concurrent restore or ownership handover,
When cleanup runs or is interrupted,
Then it rechecks the live deletion identity atomically, never purges restored/live content, and retries safely without extending older deletion dates.

- Preserve whole-Space/internal batch semantics, nested deletion dates, fresh restore/retrash identities, sole ownership and current recovery permissions. - Reuse ordinary startup/daily cleanup and retryable collaboration purge mechanisms; bound memory, rows and runtime. Disabled Space exposure must not route hidden Space rows through ordinary cleanup. Document safe handling of disabled owners and malformed recovery trees without revealing contents.
- Remove associated native snapshots, images, history and collaboration generations consistently; interrupted work stays retryable. Operator logs expose only counts/status. Backup retention is independent.
- Review existing retained Space Trash before activation and document how deletion timestamps determine eligibility; never silently reset its age to manufacture a new window.
