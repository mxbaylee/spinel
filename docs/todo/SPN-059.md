# 🔁 SPN-059 Move every document onto Spinel's sync and remove Univer collaboration

## Story

As the [Owner](../product/personas.md#owner), I want Sheets and Docs to use Spinel's own sync, so that Univer's unlicensed limits or a change on their side can't take away editing together, and any work I choose to retain survives the transition.

## Scenarios

### Retained documents open unchanged

Given retained documents, checkpoints, named versions and restore generations stored by Univer collaboration,
When the documented pre-launch transition completes,
Then each retained document opens with identical content and history and stays editable by two people with presence.

### An interrupted migration

Given a transition that converts stored data is interrupted,
When it is retried,
Then finished documents aren't converted twice, unfinished ones resume safely, and the source remains recoverable until verification succeeds.

## Rules

- Replaces SPN-014's acceptance. Preserve landed session and grant checks, guest identity and revocation, checkpoint materialization, restore generations and purge handling, with their regressions. Grooming #92, 2026-10-19.
- Spinel is pre-production until SPN-038. Record which data is deliberately retained before choosing the transition. Freshly seeded disposable test databases are acceptable; do not assume a live-production dataset, mandatory automatic startup migration or zero-downtime rollout. Never silently discard work selected for retention. Owner, chat 2026-10-21.
- For retained data, use public materialized snapshots and preserve history, grants, Formula metadata and restore generations. Choose a simple explicit conversion/import or startup migration, with restart-safe progress and fencing equivalent to today's `preparing` marker. Keep and test a source copy until verification succeeds; log progress without document content.
- Remove the flag and every `@univerjs-pro/collaboration*` package. If SPN-075 has landed, also remove `@univerjs-pro/license` and add the check that fails on any `@univerjs-pro` dependency; otherwise SPN-075 does (AGENTS.md).
- Hide menus whose mutations SPN-056 can't transform (lib/sync-transform/README.md): range and sheet protection (toolbar lock, Protect range, sheet-tab Protect sheet, Change permissions and Remove protection), sheet-tab Copy for sheets of 6000 cells or more, and Calculation options. The name box, the defined-name manager and cut-and-paste to another sheet stay: SPN-056 transforms them.
- Rewrite docs/collaboration.md to describe the new design, and drop the Univer limits text (SPN-010 and SPN-013 were retired, owner chat 2026-10-19).

- Carry Formula definitions through migration without calculated values and test native formula edits. Retain MCP credential/scope/revocation, exact-base writes and retry identity at the replacement sync boundary (SPN-034/SPN-033; grooming, 2026-10-20).

## Out of scope

- New collaboration features. Offline editing (SPN-053).

## Verify

- Every existing collaboration, guest and revocation, checkpoint, restore-generation and purge regression passes against the new module.
- A long-running two-editor Playwright fuzz run finds no divergence. Test the chosen transition on a seeded source copy, including retained history/metadata and interruption; test fresh initialization separately.
