# 🗄️ SPN-125 Prune supervised restore evidence safely

## Story

As the [Operator](../product/personas.md#operator), I want old completed restore evidence removed under a reviewed policy, so that the recovery root does not fill up and restore never becomes permanently unavailable.

## Scenarios

### Completed restores accumulate

Given many committed or recovered restores, each retaining its encrypted source and raw/frozen custody,
When retention runs or the Operator starts a new preparation,
Then evidence for resolved restores past the policy is removed oldest first, with its journal record,
And unresolved, ambiguous or in-flight evidence is never removed, and the 65th-ID refusal no longer blocks a healthy workspace.

## Rules

- Split by SPN-112's merge review, 2026-10-30: [disposition](../implementation/SPN-112.md#merge-review-dispositions). SPN-112 keeps everything and documents the 64-ID bound and capacity planning in `docs/backups.md`.
- Removal is a supervisor-owned, journaled operation with its own crash cuts, never a file deletion by the application or an operator shortcut.
- Persona weighing: the Operator needs bounded disk and an always-available restore; nobody may lose the evidence an unresolved recovery depends on.

## Out of scope

- Changing what a restore retains while it is unresolved; backup catalog retention (SPN-109).

## Verify

- Supervisor tests: pruning order, refusal for unresolved/ambiguous records, interruption at each durable step, and a 65th restore succeeding after pruning.
- `docs/backups.md` and `docs/maintenance.md` describe the policy and capacity.
