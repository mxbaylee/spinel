# 📵 SPN-070 Remove a lost device's passkey and sign it out

## Story

As a [Collaborator](../product/personas.md#collaborator), I want to remove one of my own passkeys and sign out my other devices from Your profile, so that a lost laptop loses access right away without waiting for the [Operator](../product/personas.md#operator). The [Owner](../product/personas.md#owner) gets the same.

## Scenarios

### Removing a passkey

Given Your profile lists my three passkeys,
When I choose **More → Remove** on one and confirm,
Then that credential is deleted, its sessions end, and its open editors disconnect.

### Signing out everywhere else

Given I'm signed in on my phone and a lost laptop,
When I choose **Sign out other devices** on the phone,
Then every other session of mine ends, and the phone stays signed in.

### Keeping a way in

Given only one passkey is left,
When I try to remove it,
Then I'm told to add another first, and nothing is removed.

## Rules

- SPN-047 handoff: Profile → Passkeys: named device rows, last use and More; no separate full-width card per key. Follow [the composed design](../design/account.md) and decision #126 (2026-10-20); preserve current behavior and owner screenshot approval.

- Same revocation path as the admin's **Remove** (docs/admin.md): attributed and unattributed sessions, editor disconnection and the race with an in-flight sign-in (#58).
- Your own last passkey can't be removed by yourself; recovery stays with the Operator (invitations.md).
- Requires a fresh passkey check (user verification within the last five minutes), so a stolen unlocked session can't lock the owner out.
- Fills the "passkey row More → Remove" gap in docs/design/account.md. Lives on Your profile (SPN-045).

- Distinguish browser-session sign-out from Disconnect for assistant connections; preserve connection/PAT management and pending OAuth consent through the profile flow (SPN-034; grooming, 2026-10-20).

## Out of scope

- Renaming passkeys. Admin-side removal, which exists.

## Verify

- Unit: own-key scope, the last-key refusal, sessions ended, and a stale verification refused.
- Playwright: two browser contexts; remove one context's passkey from the other and check it is signed out and its editor locks.
- Cover the existing revocation race: the shared-items poll can receive 401 and redirect to sign-in before an assertion observes the editor's transient “Access changed” status. Assert the intended final revocation behavior, and make the admin fixture clean up passkeys/users or use unique IDs per attempt so retries cannot fail on duplicate credentials. Seen in [SPN-033 CI run 39488000849, development job 118853376082](https://github.com/mxbaylee/spinel/actions/runs/39488000849/job/118853376082); the affected auth, Sheet editor, polling and admin test code was unchanged by SPN-033.
