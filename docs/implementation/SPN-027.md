## Legacy decision 66: Canonical colors and appearance

**Recorded:** 2026-10-18. **Status:** superseded in part.
**Authority:** Agent appearance implementation; later owner design choices.
**Source:** [original decision #66](https://github.com/mxbaylee/spinel/issues/66).

**Question:** How should Spinel's themes follow system preferences while keeping collaborative
document data stable?

**Options considered in the original source:**

A — device-local system/light/dark preference and display-only adaptation of the sixteen canonical
foreground/background preset pairs. B — rewrite stored colors whenever a viewer switches themes,
which creates shared edits and conflicting preferences. C — introduce custom cell metadata and
command interception for every native fill/font edit, expanding the persistence contract
substantially.

**Outcome, rationale and current boundary:**

Store canonical native colors and derive appearance per viewer so theme changes do not edit shared
content. The original 16 light/dark preset pairs and custom-pair/native behavior describe the
historical implementation. The later white-paper proposal and extra fill controls are superseded by
approved native dark appearance in decision
[#126](SPN-047.md#legacy-decision-126-approved-redesign-and-its-limits) and subsequent fill cleanup.
[SPN-088's requested eight extra named choices](SPN-088.md#retain-native-colors-and-retire-the-extra-palette-proposal) were explicitly cancelled in 2026-10-23 grooming, not
delivered by the native palette. Slides were removed by decision
[#104](SPN-074.md#legacy-decision-104-remove-unsupported-slides). Print/raster color parity is not
established by editor screenshots.

**Persona tradeoffs recorded at the time:**

The Owner wants the approved gem-rail design and readable, familiar fills. The Collaborator needs
their own appearance preference without another person's theme change editing shared work. The
Operator needs local presentation changes to leave data/history and access controls untouched, with
no external font/CDN dependency. The Link guest should receive readable system-default styling
without an account or personal metadata access.

**Source chronology:**

- [Comment 6252903745](https://github.com/mxbaylee/spinel/issues/66#issuecomment-6252903745): Rendered evidence led to a public-API white Sheet canvas and accessible Doc typing instruction; stored colors were unchanged. Retry-only CI still failed the flaky gate. The later native dark design supersedes the white-paper direction.
