## Retention guidance in the download result

2026-10-29, agent judgment under the standing four-persona delegation.

**Question:** How does a person retain downloads when the browser does not grant
persistent storage? Existing download preparation requests and reports the real
Storage API result, but its confirmation lacks the promised help action.

**Decision:** Reuse the production device-storage explanation from every download
confirmation, with a direct **How to keep them** action for unprotected storage.
Explain ordinary profiles, clearing data, reconnecting to synchronize and the
browsers' retention limits. Keep help in place so unsynchronized editor work is
not interrupted. Installation is optional and never a requirement for this ticket.

**Alternatives:** Duplicated editor/Space guidance would drift; requiring a visit
to Profile would interrupt the immediate task. Browser-specific persistence
claims without observed results would promise more than the platform guarantees.

**Personas:** Owner and Collaborator gain immediate retention guidance and keep
their edits in place. Operator gains accurate support information without access
to private documents or a new service. Link guests retain their existing online
boundary and gain no private catalog or offline privilege.
