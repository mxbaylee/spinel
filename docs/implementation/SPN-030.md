## Legacy decision 34: Backup destination and temporary drill

**Recorded:** 2026-10-18. **Status:** accepted, historical scoped authorization.
**Authority:** Owner, chat 2026-10-17.
**Source:** [original decision #34](https://github.com/mxbaylee/spinel/issues/34).

**Question:** Which offsite destination should SPN-030 use for encrypted nightly backups?

**Options considered in the original source:**

A — DigitalOcean Spaces. B — Amazon S3 or an existing S3-compatible bucket. C — defer destination
and live restore evidence until public redeployment, keeping this ticket open. No resource creation
or charges are authorized by the choice alone.

**Outcome, rationale and current boundary:**

The owner ultimately chose DigitalOcean Spaces and authorized temporary resources through their
signed-in browser for a reviewed backup/restore drill, followed by destruction. That final recorded
choice resolves the earlier pending provider text. Encryption, retention and fresh-directory restore
tooling required review first; credentials and recovery keys stay out of chat. This is the
historical create/test/destroy authorization for that drill, not standing permission to create
resources or spend again. Public redeployment and current resource choices remain separately
owner-controlled; the original online-backup method is superseded by decision
[#68](SPN-025.md#legacy-decision-68-single-sqlite-runtime-and-consistent-copies).

**Persona tradeoffs recorded at the time:**

The Operator needs a destination they can administer and a recoverable key stored separately from
the server. The Owner needs tested recovery of documents and history after machine loss. The
Collaborator needs the same protection for shared work without exposing it to the storage provider.
The Link guest receives no backup access or broader document discovery.

## Legacy decision 36: Encrypted nightly recovery bundle

**Recorded:** 2026-10-18. **Status:** superseded in part.
**Authority:** Agent backup implementation judgment.
**Source:** [original decision #36](https://github.com/mxbaylee/spinel/issues/36).

**Question:** How should SPN-030 produce recoverable nightly Spaces backups without extra services?

**Options considered in the original source:**

A — SQLite online backup, encrypted recovery metadata in the copy, gzip plus AES-256-GCM with a
separately retained random key, private Spaces objects and calendar retention; B — external backup
agent/archive service with additional operational state.

**Outcome, rationale and current boundary:**

Run in-process at 02:00 UTC with startup catch-up; retain newest per 7 daily, 4 Monday-based weekly
and 6 monthly periods plus the newest good backup regardless of age. Verify uploads before pruning.
Encrypt compressed content and recovery metadata with AES-256-GCM and a separately retained random
key; include APP_ORIGIN, immutable image and runtime environment. Stage plaintext only on the
encrypted mount and restore to a new directory after authentication/SQLite checks. No
secrets/content enter admin status. Decision
[#68](SPN-025.md#legacy-decision-68-single-sqlite-runtime-and-consistent-copies) replaces the
online-backup API with consistent VACUUM INTO; full-database recovery and private Spaces storage
remain the historical contract.

**Persona tradeoffs recorded at the time:**

Operator benefits from a streaming, in-process daily job and a single recovery bundle; Owner gets
consistent snapshots, images and future history tables; Collaborator retains grants and credentials;
Link guest retains access boundaries because backups stay private and authenticated encryption
prevents substitution.
