## Legacy decision 58: Credential-specific revocation

**Recorded:** 2026-10-18. **Status:** accepted.
**Authority:** Agent account-management judgment.
**Source:** [original decision #58](https://github.com/mxbaylee/spinel/issues/58).

**Question:** How should passkey-specific revocation handle existing sessions and operator lockout?

**Options considered in the original source:**

A — bind new sessions, revoke the chosen key and its sessions plus that user's unattributed legacy
sessions, preserve other attributable sessions, disable users without deleting ownership, and
prevent self-disable or removal of an active admin's last key; B — sign out every device on every
revocation and permit operator lockout; C — retain unattributed sessions, leaving revoked devices
potentially active.

**Outcome, rationale and current boundary:**

Bind new sessions to the passkey used. Revoking a passkey invalidates its sessions and legacy
unattributed sessions, while preserving unrelated device sessions; explain synced-key consequences.
Revalidate active-account/session authority on live paths. Account disable preserves owned documents
and shares for later deliberate recovery; do not allow an admin to disable themselves or remove
their last sign-in key. Account administration never gives the Operator document access.

The maintained [administration rules](../admin.md) specify revocation timing,
legacy sessions, pending invitations and the distinction between account recovery
and content ownership handover.

**Persona tradeoffs recorded at the time:**

Operator needs immediate revocation without losing the only administrative login; Owner retains
documents and shares when disabled; Collaborator keeps access through their own independent account
while revoked devices lose both HTTP and live editing; Link guest gains no administration API or
user/device metadata.
