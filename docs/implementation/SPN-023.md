## Legacy decision 39: History retention spacing

**Recorded:** 2026-10-18. **Status:** accepted.
**Authority:** Owner, chat 2026-10-17.
**Source:** [original decision #39](https://github.com/mxbaylee/spinel/issues/39).

**Question:** Confirm the history retention schedule?

**Options considered in the original source:**

A — Use the approved schedule. B — choose a different scope or timing, with additional
implementation and validation cost.

**Outcome, rationale and current boundary:**

Retain five-minute checkpoints during the first hour plus idle checkpoints, 30-minute spacing from
one to six hours, hourly from six to 24 hours, then the final snapshot of each day. Named versions
remain indefinitely. Decision
[#51](SPN-023.md#legacy-decision-51-materialized-native-history-checkpoints) supplies the concrete
capture/materialization policy. Personal pin removal in decision
[#128](SPN-047.md#legacy-decision-128-remove-personal-pinning) does not remove named history
versions or their retention.

**Persona tradeoffs recorded at the time:**

Operator favours predictable maintenance and cost; Owner favours preserved documents and reliable
daily access; Collaborator favours continuity and permissions; Link guest needs isolation and timely
revocation.

## Legacy decision 51: Materialized native history checkpoints

**Recorded:** 2026-10-18. **Status:** accepted.
**Authority:** Agent history implementation judgment.
**Source:** [original decision #51](https://github.com/mxbaylee/spinel/issues/51).

**Question:** Should Spinel keep its own compressed checkpoints to implement the approved decay
policy?

**Options considered in the original source:**

A — keep application-owned compressed native checkpoints materialized through public collaboration
APIs, retaining image bytes and using the approved decay schedule; B — integrate the segment index
and still add a separate checkpoint/pruning store to meet the ticket.

**Outcome, rationale and current boundary:**

Store compressed application-native checkpoints materialized through public APIs, using a 30-second
scheduler and one-minute idle boundary with the approved spacing and UTC daily retention. Capture
before legacy overwrite, namespace revisions and recheck access across asynchronous compression.
Keep pending/rejected edits out of confirmed history and named versions recoverable. A storage
budget is not a claim that the SDK operation log is capped; history and collaboration retention are
separate mechanisms.

**Persona tradeoffs recorded at the time:**

Operator needs bounded checkpoint growth in the existing backed-up SQLite file; Owner needs recent
states and idle breaks preserved; Collaborator needs the same confirmed shared state, with rejected
or pending edits excluded; Link guest must not gain a history endpoint or bypass document grants.
