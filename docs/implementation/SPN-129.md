Options considered: finish the XL system; attempt another workflow split with
conservative full reruns; or retire the optimization. Choose retirement because
no small, verified alternative has been demonstrated and further platform work
is disproportionate to this two-person workspace's goal. This does not claim
that simpler designs are impossible or that retirement makes existing CI faster.

The owner-authorized cancellation supersedes SPN-115's retained optimization
requirement and SPN-129's later acceptance/setup plans. Those outcomes are
withdrawn, not completed or silently deferred into follow-ups. Do not create the
