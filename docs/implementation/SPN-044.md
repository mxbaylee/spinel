## Legacy decision 109: Automatic document-specific preview cards

**Recorded:** 2026-10-20. **Status:** accepted; generic-only recommendation superseded.
**Authority:** Owner, chat 2026-10-20.
**Source:** [original decision #109](https://github.com/mxbaylee/spinel/issues/109).

**Question:** What information should guest-link social previews show?

**Options considered in the original source:**

A — generic previews for all pages; B — automatically show title and Document/Spreadsheet type on
enabled guest links, while private pages stay generic; C — expose full contents.

**Outcome, rationale and current boundary:**

Automatically show title and Document/Spreadsheet type for enabled View and Edit guest links, with
no extra opt-in; private pages remain generic. Exclude contents, owner identity and collaborator
data. A separate random 256-bit query preview capability, independently hashed from the fragment
content capability, grants title/type only. Check revoke/rotate/trash/owner-disable before and after
rendering; no-store/no-referrer, two render slots and no unbounded queue/cache. Legacy fragment-only
links remain generic until replaced. External previews already fetched cannot be recalled. Reuse
existing artwork/Sharp/bundled OFL font, not a new service. The issue's generic-only title is
historical and does not override its explicit final owner decision.

**Persona tradeoffs recorded at the time:**

Operator wants bounded rendering on the small host; Owner wants attractive automatic
document-specific sharing; Collaborator wants contents and personal identity excluded; Link guest
wants recognizable, revocable document-scoped links.
