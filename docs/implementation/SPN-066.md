# SPN-066 — Guest-link inventory and expiry

## Owner inventory and terminal expiry

**Accepted — agent judgment, 2026-10-28**, under the owner's standing
[decision delegation](grooming-2026-10-26.md#agent-decisions-and-visual-review).
The original ticket requires one owner-wide list, optional expiry, and the same
privacy boundary as turning a link off. Existing per-item sharing and the
approved [profile inventory design](../design/sharing.md) supply the controls.

The inventory lives at **Your profile → Guest links** and contains only the
signed-in everyday account's own, available standalone Docs/Sheets and outer
Spaces. A Space appears once with its all-current-and-future-contents scope.
Rows expose title, type, confirmed role, recorded enable date and deadline;
they never contain a guest URL, token or preview capability. Existing links
keep their role, epoch and capability hashes. Their historical enable date
remains unknown and their default expiry is never.

An active link's deadline can be changed to never, one day, seven days or 30
days from the confirmed update. This preserves its address. An expired link
remains visible but cannot be revived by extending its deadline: the owner must
create a fresh link through the item's Share dialog. Turning it off removes the
row. This chooses a terminal expired capability over silently re-enabling an
address that may have escaped its intended audience.

Profile actions carry the observed link epoch so a stale page cannot change or
turn off a newer replacement. Uncertain writes refresh authoritative metadata
before allowing another action. Account changes and unmounted views cannot
accept delayed responses. Operator and offline profiles make no inventory
request.

All guest authorization boundaries reject at the deadline independently of
cleanup. Cleanup also fences the epoch, clears guest sessions and closes the
root's guest connections, including Space children. Existing authoritative
revocation hides clean and dirty editors, titles and actions; dirty input gets
only the established generic unsaved warning. A network failure alone remains
nonterminal and private-account recovery is unchanged.

### Persona effects and tradeoffs

- **Owner:** one truthful inventory and bounded sharing without changing active
  addresses; an expired address requires a deliberate fresh share.
- **Collaborator:** existing account grants and delegated File admin sharing
  remain intact; the profile inventory does not disclose another owner's files.
- **Link guest:** access stops at the intended deadline across content, writes,
  images and social previews, with the established privacy-first unsaved warning.
- **Operator:** no document titles or new external service; a small in-process
  cleanup uses the same durable access boundary and application lifecycle.
