## Legacy decision 49: Document-bound WebSocket admission

**Recorded:** 2026-10-18. **Status:** accepted.
**Authority:** Agent access-boundary judgment.
**Source:** [original decision #49](https://github.com/mxbaylee/spinel/issues/49).

**Question:** Should every live connection be bound to one authorized document before upgrade, with
content-free logs for rejected edits?

**Options considered in the original source:**

A — check current access to the requested document before upgrade, bind ticket and upgrade
cookie/scope in the public connect hook before HELLO/JOIN, enforce scope on JOIN and presence,
retain current-role checks on reads/writes, and log denial category plus validated UUIDs only; B —
leave document checks until JOIN, which does not meet the pre-upgrade acceptance requirement.

**Outcome, rationale and current boundary:**

Before HTTP 101, verify session and requested document grant; after upgrade, validate exact
connection scope before HELLO. Bind tickets/cookies to the document and current credential, reject
Viewer writes and cross-document reuse, and recheck current grants. Denial logs contain useful
metadata without document content or credential material. Guest connection behavior must retain its
own scoped identity and never inherit an account dashboard privilege.

**Persona tradeoffs recorded at the time:**

Operator needs useful denial evidence without document content or credentials in logs; Owner needs
no cross-document leakage or viewer writes; Collaborator needs current grants and clear read-only
behavior; Link guest needs a future token bound to just its document, with no account fallback.
