# 🔌 SPN-161 Verify revoked socket closure across navigation

## Story

As the [Owner](../product/personas.md#owner), I want revoking one login to prove that its actual connection closed even while it leaves the document, so that another login's queued work stays safe and later broadcasts cannot reach the revoked login.

## Scenarios

### Revocation remains provable when navigation wins

Given two established logins and a queued native edit in the other login,
When one login is revoked while its page navigates,
Then the actual frozen connection identities have a physical-close witness and the revoked login receives 401,
And the other login retains its socket, queued edit and exact saved/reloaded native value, with no later frame reaching the revoked connection.

### Missing closure or authority never passes

Given an absent, wrong or stale close, a live socket plus 401, a closed socket plus 200, an unauthorized reopen, observer loss/overflow or a later broadcast,
When the revocation assertion runs,
Then it rejects the incomplete evidence; navigation, a close-method call and cleanup-only closure cannot satisfy it,
And the first failure and bounded receipt are retained before owned cleanup, whose result is recorded separately.
